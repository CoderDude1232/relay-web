Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Relay is built around a simple principle: your conversations are yours. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

What we collect

We collect the minimum necessary to operate the service:

  • Username — chosen by you at registration, used to identify your account.
  • Account credentials — a derived password stored as a bcrypt hash. We never see your plaintext password.
  • Public encryption keys — your Signal Protocol prekeys, stored so other users can establish encrypted sessions with you. These are public by design.
  • Encrypted message envelopes — temporarily stored on the server until delivered to the recipient. The server cannot read the content.
  • APNs device token — used to send push notification pings. Push payloads contain only the encrypted ciphertext; they do not contain message content in plaintext.

What we do not collect

  • Your phone number, email address, or real name
  • Your contacts or address book
  • Your location
  • Message content — all messages are end-to-end encrypted and the server only ever handles ciphertext
  • Analytics or behavioural data
  • Advertising identifiers

End-to-end encryption

Every message sent through Relay is encrypted on your device before it reaches our servers, using the Signal Protocol (PQXDH key exchange + Double Ratchet). Only you and your intended recipient hold the keys needed to decrypt messages. We are technically unable to read your messages even if compelled to.

Data retention

Message envelopes are deleted from our servers as soon as they are delivered and acknowledged by the recipient's device. Undelivered messages are queued for a limited period and then purged. Attachment blobs are deleted after 30 days.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or analytics purposes. We may disclose data if required by law, but because message content is encrypted, we cannot provide readable message content regardless.

Your data, your control

You can delete your account at any time from the app's Settings screen. This permanently removes your account, keys, and any queued messages from our servers.

Security

All connections between the Relay app and our servers use TLS with certificate pinning. The local message database on your device is encrypted with SQLCipher, keyed by a secret stored in the Secure Enclave.
